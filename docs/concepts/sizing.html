Sizing

Before build, size decides who works. The size: label and how complete the brief is route each task to a tier. Bigger tasks get extra planning passes first.

The fully-specified test

A size:s task, a size:m peripheral piece or a correction fix list goes to the micro tier only when its brief passes the fully-specified test: every file is named, the behaviour and the tests are exact, and no approach is left to choose. A micro build stops on any choice the brief did not make. Anything that needs judgement goes to the small tier instead (briefing.md).

Planning passes

From size:m up, a read-only brief falsifier checks the brief before anything is built, and a brief-gaps verdict blocks the build. A size:m task is decomposed into a design-bearing core and peripheral pieces. size:l and size:xl tasks first get plan validation and solution research, each a survey followed by a synthesis. The research memo, not code, is what those passes produce (planning-research.md).

Size budgets

Each size also carries a cost budget: xs $2, s $8, m $30, l $90 and xl $250. When a task's spend passes three times its budget, Marvin pauses and asks you (escalation.md). Without the telemetry plugin, spend is unknown rather than zero, and that signal cannot fire (token-economics.md).
