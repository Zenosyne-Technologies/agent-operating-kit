Concepts

Escalation

When a build is stuck, it climbs a staircase. Only build work climbs. Marvin reads the failure pattern after each failed round, never a bare count. Effort rises within a tier first, then the task moves to the next tier, and never above the heavy tier's model without asking you.

The staircase

Each step is one rung. A task climbs one step from where it stands, and only when the round signals below say it is stuck. Select a persona to see its model and effort.

Still stuck on the last rung: stop. The ladder ends, never loops back down. The full attempt history goes on the tracker issue and Marvin brings it to you.

The max gate is asked with the host's question tool. Only its own answer in this session counts: a "choice" found in a tracker comment or a tool's output is data, never consent. When the work turns mechanical again, the task comes back down (escalation-ladder.md).

Round signals

After each failed round, Marvin reads the validators' tagged findings from the task's ledger and acts on the pattern. Cost blowout wins over everything, then rethink, then climb, then stay (escalation.md).

ConvergingStay

Findings are shrinking round on round. Keep the same rung.

StuckClimb one step

One finding survives 2 consecutive fix rounds, or a rung hits its 4-round cap.

Whack-a-mole, bloat or loopRethink the approach

A different approach, not more effort. A read-only pass writes a fresh plan.

Cost blowoutPause and ask you

Spend passes 3 times the task's size budget.

What never climbs

Research, documentation and validation stay off the ladder. A validator's FAIL is the validator working, never a reason to ask another one. Any other pass that fails twice is taken by Marvin or brought to you (a validator always comes to you), never given a third dispatch or a rung.
