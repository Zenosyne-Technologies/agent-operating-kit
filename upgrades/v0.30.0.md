# v0.30.0 — the orchestrator's model is the ceiling; escalate effort, not model

Consumer-facing: **dispatch defaults change.** Heavy workers no longer inherit the session's effort, and the frontier model is no longer the automatic escalation target.

- `claude plugin update marvin` — adds four escalation personas (`marvin:escalation-high`/`-xhigh`/`-max`, and `-frontier`) and pins `effort: medium` on `marvin:developer`, `marvin:researcher` and the three validators (previously they inherited the session's effort, so a frontier session at high/max ran every heavy worker at high/max).
- **Re-sync CLAUDE.md's kit-authored lines with the current template** — CLAUDE.md is merged, never refreshed, so a merge alone leaves it contradicting the refreshed guides. Three changed; keep your own rules untouched:
  - the **Model-tier dispatch** section: the `Orchestrator (…)` line now names the HEAVY-worker model (e.g. Opus), not the frontier model, and the old "two failed attempts → escalate to the frontier model" line becomes the effort ladder with the ask-at-max frontier gate (map the four tier placeholders by role label, per the upgrade skill's step 4b);
  - the **Task lifecycle** FAIL-return sentence: a FAIL returns the task to its current rung on the ladder, never back down, counting as one of that rung's two attempts;
  - the **Standing rules** milestone-validation clause: led by the orchestrator on the heavy-worker model, not the frontier model.
- The step-4 refresh re-renders `.marvin/agents/`: `escalation.md` (new — the ladder), `guardrails.md`, `validation-agent.md`, `token-economics.md`, `reporting.md`, and the tracker's `stats-collection-brief.md`. Customised copies are backed up first.
- Stats snapshots move to **schema v4**: cost tiers are assigned by ROLE, not model, and `by_tier` gains a `ladder` bucket with a per-rung split. Snapshots already written stay as they are — a digest trend comparing a v3 snapshot's tiers with a v4 one crosses that change, so read the first post-upgrade tier trend with care.
- Recommended: run your orchestrator session on the heavy-worker model (e.g. Opus) at `medium` effort — it is now the ceiling.
